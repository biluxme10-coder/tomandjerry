Character 🔒

Tom

A blue-grey house cat with a full-time job and a 0% success rate at it. Has been flattened, exploded, and folded into an accordion more times than HR would allow.

Character 🔒

Jerry

A brown house mouse with excellent real-estate instincts — he has lived rent-free inside a wall for nine decades and has never once been evicted.

Character 🔒

Spike & Tyke

A bulldog with exactly one rule: don't wake the baby. Tom breaks this rule in nearly every episode he appears in, on purpose, somehow.

Trivia 🔒

The first chase

"Puss Gets the Boot" premiered in 1940. Jerry wasn't named Jerry yet. Nobody involved thought there'd be a second one, let alone two hundred.

Trivia 🔒

Seven Oscars

The Tom and Jerry shorts won seven Academy Awards for Best Animated Short Film — more than any other character-based theatrical cartoon series.

Object 🔒

The frying pan

Deployed in roughly every third episode. Structural integrity: questionable. Comedic reliability: perfect. Insurance would never cover this household.

Character 🔒

Nibbles / Tuffy

A tiny orphaned mouse who eats his body weight in cheese per episode and somehow still needs Jerry to babysit him through a war zone of a kitchen.

Trivia 🔒

Almost no dialogue

Most classic shorts run on sound effects and orchestral score instead of spoken lines — one of the few cartoons that translates perfectly with the volume up and the subtitles off.
